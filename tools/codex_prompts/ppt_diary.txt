Prop illustration for a game concept presentation. The attached image is the game's sewing room, for the art style and colors.
A dark wooden cork pin board hangs on a gothic wallpapered wall, lit by a single candle. Seven torn old diary pages are pinned to it in a loose row with sewing pins and brass tacks, connected by a red thread. From left to right the pages degrade: the first pages are clean cream parchment with neat faint handwriting scribbles and a tiny drawing of a mouse child; the middle pages are crumpled, stained, the scribbles get larger and wilder; the last pages are soaked with dark crimson blood stains, torn edges, frantic scratched scribbles. On the margin of each page a small separate line of neat pencil scribble (the daughter's note). The scribbles must be abstract squiggle lines only, not readable letters.
Art style: Tim Burton-like gothic, Don't Starve Together-like hand-drawn 2D, cut-out paper look, rough consistent-weight thick black outlines, paper-grain texture. Low saturation, low brightness, plum and grey-violet shadows, honey candle highlights (#C9A46A), crimson (#C62E2E) blood.
